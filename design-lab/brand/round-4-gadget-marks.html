i3City · brand plan · round 4

Marks drawn from the gadgets themselves

Each logo now starts from something customers actually hold: an earbud, the camera on the back of a phone, the phone itself. The name sits beside it in i3City, in the font the site already uses, so only the symbol changes between options. The pinks stay sharp.

Where each shape comes from

  • An earbud is a round head on a thin stem, which already looks like a lowercase i.
  • Modern phones carry three camera lenses, and the 3 in the name can be those lenses.
  • The shop sells phones and people carry them home, so a phone can double as a shopping bag.

What it still has to do

  • Read as a 16px browser tab icon.
  • Crop to a circle for the WhatsApp Business profile.
  • Work in the light and dark themes of the site.
  • Print in one colour on a sticker or box.

Rollout

From decision to a live brand

  1. Decide

    Pick a direction and a tagline. A few options: "Original gadgets. Delivered nationwide." / "Gadgets from Egbeda, to your door." / "Everything your phone needs."

  2. Draw the master files

    Produce the full lockup, the standalone symbol, a one-colour version, a reversed version for dark backgrounds, a 512px app icon with safe padding, a 1200 by 630 share image and a round-safe WhatsApp avatar. Keep each as SVG with a PNG export.

  3. Update the design system first

    The new pinks go into the tokens before any component uses them, then get documented. Add a Brand section to DESIGN.md covering clear space, minimum size and what not to do, and replace the note that calls the wordmark a placeholder.

    apps/web/app/tokens.css · DESIGN.md

  4. Ship it in the product

    Build one shared Logo component and use it in the storefront header and footer, the admin sidebar and sign-in page, the email templates and the packing slip. Regenerate the favicon, Apple icon, manifest icons and default share image from the new artwork.

    apps/web/components · apps/web/app/icon.tsx · apps/web/app/opengraph-image.tsx · apps/web/app/manifest.ts

  5. Carry it offline and into chat

    Set the WhatsApp Business profile photo and description, update the Instagram avatar, and print order stickers and a thank-you card for each parcel. Match the shop signage in Egbeda last, once the digital version has settled.

  6. Check and lock

    Run the Lighthouse mobile check that is still open in the PRD, confirm the icon in light and dark browser tabs, and share the link on WhatsApp to see the preview card. Then freeze the files.
