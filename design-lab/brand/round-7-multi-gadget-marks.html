i3City · logo mark

A whole shop of gadgets, one mark

The name is now plain text in the site font, set as i3City, with no styling. The logo is the shape beside it: three ways of putting several gadgets into one mark, drawn in every purple and pink of the palette. The icon, the WhatsApp avatar and the header all use the same mark.

Variant A

Skyline

A city made of gadgets

i3City
i3City

The idea

A power bank, a smartwatch, a phone, an earbud case and a speaker stand side by side like buildings in a skyline, with the phone as the tallest tower. i3City becomes a city of gadgets, which is the name drawn as a picture. Every purple and pink in the palette appears once.

Good

  • It tells the whole story: the range and the name together.
  • Colourful and busy in a good way, so it never looks dull.
  • The phone screen with its little coloured apps gives the mark a focal point.

Watch out for

  • It is the widest mark, so the phone header uses a smaller version.
  • The icon uses only the middle three gadgets, because five do not fit a square.

Variant B

Sticker

Gadgets tumbling out of a round badge

i3City
i3City

The idea

A phone, a watch and an earbud tumble together inside a soft round badge, with a pink sparkle. It looks like a sticker you would put on a phone case, and it works well as a round social avatar.

Good

  • Friendly and young, which suits accessories and gifts.
  • Already round, so the WhatsApp photo crop never cuts it.
  • Lends itself to being printed as an actual sticker in each parcel.

Watch out for

  • The busiest drawing, so it turns into a pink blob at 16px.
  • Feels less like a serious electronics shop.

Variant C

Grid

A home screen of gadgets

i3City
i3City

The idea

Four rounded tiles in four colours, each holding one gadget: a phone, a watch, an earbud and a charge bolt. It looks like the home screen of a phone, which makes the shop feel like where gadgets live.

Good

  • The strongest at small sizes, since four colour blocks stay clear even at 16px.
  • Easy to grow. A tile can be swapped for a seasonal gadget or a new category.
  • The most modern and tidy of the three.

Watch out for

  • It looks a bit like a generic app icon, and tells less of the story behind the name.
