i3City · logo mark

A watch and an earbud, one mark

The name is now plain text in the site font, set as i3City, with no styling. The logo is the shape beside it: three different ways of combining a smartwatch with an earbud, drawn in the Orchid colours. The icon, the WhatsApp avatar and the header all use the same mark.

Variant A

Screen

An earbud on the watch display

i3City
i3City

The idea

A smartwatch seen from the front, with an earbud shown on its screen like an app. It tells the story in one look: the shop sells watches and earbuds. The pink earbud also forms a soft "i" shape against the orchid case.

Good

  • The most literal and the easiest to explain to anyone.
  • The watch outline works on its own as the favicon.
  • Pink on orchid keeps it inside the new brand colours.

Watch out for

  • The most detail of the three, so the earbud disappears at 16px and only the watch shape remains.

Variant B

Hand

The earbud is the hour hand

i3City
i3City

The idea

A round watch where the hour hand is an earbud: the head sits at the centre of the dial and the stem points to the hour. One simple silhouette, with the second idea hidden inside it for people to discover.

Good

  • The cleanest and most elegant of the three, with a single recognisable shape.
  • The simplest shape of the three, so it should hold up best small. Check it in a real browser tab at 16px.
  • Looks like a premium brand mark rather than an icon collage.

Watch out for

  • The earbud is subtle, and at some angles the hand looks like a number 6. Some people will see only a watch until it is pointed out.

Variant C

Pair

A watch and an earbud side by side

i3City
i3City

The idea

A watch and an earbud overlapping, in two colours. The earbud is pink and sits in front, with a thin cut-out line separating it from the watch. This is the most direct reading of "a watch combined with airpods".

Good

  • Both products are instantly visible and in their own colour.
  • The two-tone pairing suits the orchid and pink palette well.
  • Extends naturally to a pattern for packaging, with watches and earbuds repeated.

Watch out for

  • Two objects make it the widest mark, so it needs more room in the phone header.
