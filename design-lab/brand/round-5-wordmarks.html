i3City · wordmark

Just the name, made unmistakable

A wordmark only, in the Orchid colours, written i3City. Each one is a real typeface turned into a custom logo shape: the dots on the i letters are redrawn, the letters are spaced by hand, and "i3" is orchid while "City" is deep plum. The icon in the corner is the "i3" pair on its own. The three typefaces are open licence, and the logo is outlined, so the site does not need to load a font for it.

Variant A

Spark

Wide, confident letters with sparkling dots

The idea

Syne is a wide, quirky display face that almost no shop uses. The dots on both i letters become four-point sparks in pink, like the glint on a new screen. "i3" is orchid, "City" is deep plum, so the name reads as a brand and a place.

Good

  • The widest and most striking of the three, so it is recognisable from far away.
  • The sparks are a small detail that survives well as the dot of the icon.
  • Works as a stamp or sticker in one colour.

Watch out for

  • Wide letters take more header width on phones, so the phone header uses a smaller size.

Variant B

Soft

Heavy, rounded serif with round pink dots

The idea

Fraunces in its heaviest, softest setting. The curves are warm and chunky, and the pink round dots on the i letters look like little buttons. It looks friendly and a bit premium, and it stands out because gadget shops rarely use a serif.

Good

  • The most approachable and personal, which suits a shop that chats with its customers on WhatsApp.
  • The most compact, so it fits the phone header at a larger, more readable size.
  • Pairs well with the rounded pill shapes already in the app.

Watch out for

  • Reads as lifestyle more than technical. Good for accessories and audio, less so if you want the serious electronics feel.

Variant C

Power

The first i is a power button

The idea

Anybody set at its widest and boldest, a sporty tech look. The dot on the first i is a pink power symbol, the universal sign for switching a gadget on. "i3" literally powers up the name. The second i keeps a plain dot, so the idea appears once.

Good

  • The only one with a gadget idea built into the letters.
  • The power symbol links to every product the shop sells, from phones to fans to consoles.
  • Looks fast and modern, with an energetic feel.

Watch out for

  • The power dot is small at 16px, so the icon relies on the shape of "i3" rather than the symbol.
