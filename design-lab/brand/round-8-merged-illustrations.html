Gadgets merged into one illustration

Each logo is now a single square illustration where the gadgets are fused into one object, not placed side by side. The name stays plain text beside it. Because the illustration has its own rounded square background, the same artwork works as the app icon, the favicon and (cropped to a circle) the WhatsApp photo.

Variant A

Headphone phone

One object wearing three gadgets

i3City
i3City

The idea

A phone with headphones clamped over its sides and a watch strap buckled around its middle. The ear cups, the band and the strap are all parts of the phone, so three gadgets become one object. The strap has three holes, a quiet nod to the 3 in the name, and a pink sparkle adds a lift.

Good

  • Anyone can read it in a second: a phone, headphones, a strap.
  • The headphone arch gives it a bold silhouette that holds up small.
  • The strap, ear cups and sparkle give plenty of colour without clutter.

Watch out for

  • The strap covers part of the phone screen, so the screen shows only a few app tiles.

Variant B

Watch buddy

A smartwatch with earbud ears

i3City
i3City

The idea

A smartwatch whose screen is a smiling face, with an earbud on each side as ears. The straps are its hair and chin. Gadgets are fused into a single friendly character that can blink, wink or look worried when the cart is empty.

Good

  • The most memorable and the most likeable of the three.
  • Doubles as a mascot for empty states, loading screens and the order thank-you page.
  • The simple face and ears stay readable at small sizes.

Watch out for

  • Playful rather than premium, so it suits a shop for younger customers best.

Variant C

Tower

A phone as a skyscraper

i3City
i3City

The idea

A tall phone stands as a tower in a city, with a watch face as its clock, a power bank and a speaker beside it, and a headphone band arching behind like a bridge. It is the name drawn as a scene.

Good

  • Ties the picture to the City in the name.
  • Rich enough to grow into a skyline pattern for packaging.
  • The clock face in the tower ties the watch into the phone.

Watch out for

  • The least merged of the three. The side gadgets are separate, so it reads as a group.
  • More detail, so it is the busiest at small sizes.
